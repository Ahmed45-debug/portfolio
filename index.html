Why Pharm-D, and why I moved from IT

I saw how hard the body is to learn from books

When I watched medical students, I saw that many of them were trying to understand the human body only from books. That creates a lot of confusion, because a flat page does not show how the body really works.

I want to use my IT and 3D skills to make interactive 3D animations that help students understand it clearly. Studying Pharm-D will give me the knowledge to make them correctly, and as a Doctor of Pharmacy I will learn much more about medicine along the way.

I wanted a field that keeps its value

AI tools can now build websites, apps and software from a simple command. Work that used to take ten people can now be done by two or three, and fewer developers are finding jobs. I was looking for a field that will stay valuable for a long time, and I believe healthcare is one.

MBBS was very difficult

MBBS was a hard path for me, and Pharm-D is the medical field I chose. I am submitting my admission form to Iqra University now.

My journey

  1. After 10th grade

    I had a 5 to 6 month break and decided to use it to learn computer skills. I started with mobile photo editing.

  2. Finding web development

    I found website development on YouTube. It looked too hard at first and I gave up after a few videos.

  3. 5 AM, no excuses

    One morning I woke up and decided I would learn to code, no matter what. I started with HTML tags and then moved on to CSS.

  4. Learning on a phone

    I had no computer, so I practiced in the TrebEdit app on my Android phone. HTML and CSS took me about 5 to 6 months.

  5. A break, then a restart

    In 11th class I got busy and lost track. In 12th class I restarted: HTML, CSS, then JavaScript.

  6. First portfolio, live

    I built my portfolio entirely on my phone and uploaded it with a Hostinger plan. After about 1.5 years, I was a junior front-end developer.

Don't wait for perfect tools. Start where you are, use what you have.
